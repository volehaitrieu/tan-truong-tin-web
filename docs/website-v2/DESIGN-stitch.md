## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an inviting, contemporary, and dependable retail atmosphere for modern consumer electronics and home appliances in coastal Nha Trang, Vietnam. The aesthetic merges understated modern minimalism with welcoming Mediterranean coastal light, balancing technological precision with everyday warmth.

- **Brand Personality**: Approachable, reliable, authentic, technologically knowledgeable without being intimidating.
- **Target Audience**: Urban families, young professionals, tech enthusiasts, and coastal hospitality business owners seeking authentic electronics and dependable post-purchase service.
- **Visual Style**: Clean Minimalist-Warm. Elevated beyond utilitarian tech e-commerce by pairing warm white canvas tones with an electric cobalt blue signature accent. Surfaces rely on tonal separation, pill-shaped tactile controls, and soft micro-borders instead of dark dropshadows or synthetic neo-brutalist styling.
- **Emotional Intent**: Reassurance of authenticity, ease of navigation, clarity in pricing and technical specifications, and breezy coastal freshness.

## Colors
The palette abandons cold clinical tech grays in favor of an organic warm foundation illuminated by a sharp, confidence-inducing cobalt blue.

- **Canvas & Surfaces**: Base canvas is pure warm white (`#FEFCF9`), layered with nested surfaces in soft warm stone (`#F5F1EA`) and card surfaces in solid white (`#FFFFFF`). A soft border tint (`#EAE4D9`) provides structural definition without visual weight.
- **Primary Accent (`#235CE3`)**: Cobalt Blue serves as the decisive interactive and trust beacon—applied to primary checkout flows, interactive pill buttons, active filters, verified authenticity badges, and vital state indicators.
- **Neutral Palette (`#20252D`)**: High-contrast, deep slate neutral designed to ensure effortless legibility of prices, specs, and complex Vietnamese diacritical marks. Secondary typography steps down to `#5A6270` and muted utility hints use `#8C95A5`.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#E05638`)**: Coral Red handles promotional tags, discount badges, and sale banners, explicitly bypassing standard retail greens to preserve focus on the signature cobalt identity.

## Typography
**Be Vietnam Pro** is standardized across all headline, body, and label roles. Designed natively with refined diacritical marks for the Vietnamese language, it resolves vertical clipping and uneven line-height anomalies commonly found with stacked diacritics (e.g., ể, ộ, ứ, ặ).

- **Vertical Metrics**: Line heights maintain generous spacing (minimum 1.45x–1.5x on body copy) to accommodate accented Vietnamese glyphs comfortably alongside Vietnamese currency symbols (₫).
- **Price Displays**: Numeric amounts pair semibold or bold weights with tight letter tracking to emphasize value propositions while retaining rapid scanning on mobile product grids.
- **Optical Hierarchy**: Headings avoid heavy letterspacing, applying subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) only on display scales to create dense, cohesive headers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model is tailored to a mobile-first viewport centered on standard 390px-wide devices, adopting a strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm.

- **Canvas Gutters & Margins**: Outer canvas side padding is locked at `1rem` (16px) to maximize screen economy while shielding content from edge bevels. Column gutters across two-column product grids use `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Grid Structure**:
  - **Mobile (≤ 480px, base 390px)**: 4-column fluid layout or 2-column e-commerce product card grid with 12px horizontal gaps and 16px vertical row flow.
  - **Tablet (481px – 840px)**: 8-column grid with 16px gutters and 24px margins.
  - **Desktop (≥ 841px)**: 12-column grid constrained to a 1200px max-width container, 24px gutters, and auto-centered outer canvas margins.
- **Rhythm & Touch Ergonomics**: Tap targets strictly exceed 44px in height. Vertical spacing within product cards uses `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px), reserving `space-lg` (16px) for section partitions and `space-xl` (24px) between prominent promotional segments.

## Elevation & Depth
In line with the warm coastal tech aesthetic, visual hierarchy avoids heavy dropshadows, murky black blurs, and glassmorphic distortions.

- **Surface Tonal Stacking**: Depth is achieved primarily through layered background values:
  - **Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#FEFCF9` warm ground.
  - **Level 1 (Sub-section / Recessed areas)**: `#F5F1EA` soft warm stone surface for banners, specification trays, and category backings.
  - **Level 2 (Cards & Tiles)**: Solid `#FFFFFF` clean surfaces resting over Level 0 or Level 1.
- **Micro-Borders Over Harsh Shadows**: Card elevation relies on a 1px soft border in `#EAE4D9`. 
- **Ambient Accent Shadows**: When active floating elements require physical lift (e.g., sticky bottom checkout bars or floating action buttons), an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with primary cobalt is applied: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(35, 92, 227, 0.12)`. Regular interactive cards use only a soft warm glow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(32, 37, 45, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
The design embraces organic, friendly curvature to communicate warmth while preserving clean structural geometry for technical data:

- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Interactive actions, primary buttons, promotional pill chips, category selector tags, and search bars.
- **16px Curvature (`rounded-xl` / 1rem)**: Large modular cards, promotional carousel tiles, modal bottom sheets, and hero banners.
- **12px Curvature (`rounded-lg` / 0.75rem)**: Standard product catalog cards, dialog containers, and image preview enclosures.
- **8px Curvature (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem)**: Text input fields, dropdown menus, specification tables, and counter badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill**: Full pill shape (`height: 48px`), background `#235CE3`, label in bold white (`#FFFFFF`). No border. Active tap states scale smoothly to `0.98` with a subtle overlay tint.
- **Secondary Pill**: Full pill shape, background `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border `#235CE3`, text color `#235CE3`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, neutral text `#20252D`, subtle `#F5F1EA` background on press.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter Pills**: Full pill geometry (`height: 36px`, padding `0 16px`).
- **Unselected**: White (`#FFFFFF`) surface with 1px border `#EAE4D9`, text `#5A6270`.
- **Selected**: Solid `#235CE3` with white text `#FFFFFF` or soft tonal fill `#EBF0FC` with bold `#235CE3` text and border `#235CE3`.

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: 12px rounded corner container on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#EAE4D9` border. Inner content padding of 12px.
- **Image Tray**: 8px rounded `#F5F1EA` light background backing transparent PNG electronics photography.
- **Information Hierarchy**: Brand label (`body-sm`, `#8C95A5`), product title (`title-md`, max 2 lines, `#20252D`), installment tag (pill badge, `#EBF0FC`, text `#235CE3`), and bold price (`price-lg`, `#235CE3`).

### Input Fields
- **Search Bar**: 44px–48px pill-shaped bar, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#EAE4D9`, leading search icon in `#8C95A5`, text `#20252D`. Focused state transitions border to `#235CE3` with a 2px outer glow (`rgba(35, 92, 227, 0.15)`).
- **Form Controls**: 8px rounded corners, 48px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#EAE4D9`. Floating labels utilize `label-sm` in `#5A6270`.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px size with 6px rounded corners (checkboxes) or full circle (radios). Border 1.5px `#8C95A5`, transitioning to `#235CE3` background and crisp white mark when active.

### Store-Specific Badges & Sticky Bars
- **Genuine Guarantee Badge**: Pill badge with a soft shield icon, subtle warm gold-stone tint (`#F5EFE6`), text `#6E5632` verifying official Vietnamese distribution (Chính Hãng).
- **Sticky Mobile Cart Tray**: Anchored to bottom of 390px viewport, `#FFFFFF` background with 1px top border `#EAE4D9`, safe-area inset padding, displaying immediate total price on the left and full-pill `#235CE3` "Mua Ngay" (Buy Now) button on the right.
